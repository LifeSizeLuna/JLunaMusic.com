Privacy Policy

Time & a Half · Last updated August 23, 2026

Time & a Half ("the app") is built for NYC Department of Transportation Highway Repairers and their coworkers to log shifts and verify pay. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information the app processes

The app processes the following types of information, entered or scanned by you:

  • Shift dates, times, and clock-in/clock-out entries you log
  • Photos of your pay stubs, which you choose to scan
  • Leave balances (vacation, sick, comp time) extracted from your pay stubs or entered manually
  • Your job title, used to apply the correct pay rules (for example, comp-time eligibility)

Where your information is stored

All of the information above is stored locally on your device. Pay stub photos are processed using Apple's on-device Vision framework to read text directly on your iPhone — the photos and extracted data are not uploaded to any server operated by us or by any third party as part of this process.

Nothing you enter or scan is transmitted off your device unless you choose to export it yourself (for example, generating a PDF report to share).

What we don't do

  • We don't create user accounts or require sign-in
  • We don't use third-party analytics or advertising trackers
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone, because we don't have access to it in the first place
  • We never see or store your payment information — purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store

Your control over your data

You can delete all data stored by the app at any time using the "Reset All Data" option in Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data, since nothing is backed up to an external server by the app itself. (Your device's own iCloud backup settings, which are controlled by Apple and your device settings, may separately include app data — see Apple's documentation on iCloud backups for details.)

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — for example, if a future version sends pay stub text to a server for processing — this policy will be updated first, and the updated date above will reflect that change.
