Frequently asked questions

The app didn't read my pay stub correctly. What do I do?

Pay stub formats vary by agency, and scanning accuracy depends on lighting and photo angle. Try rescanning with the stub flat and well-lit. If it's still off, email us a description of what's wrong (no need to send the actual stub) and we'll work on improving recognition for that format.

Is my pay stub data sent anywhere?

No — scanning happens entirely on your device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

I bought the app but a feature seems locked.

Email us with your purchase details and which device you're using, and we'll help troubleshoot. If needed, you can also try Settings → Restore Purchases in the app first.

Can I get my leave balances or shift history back if I reset the app?

Using "Reset All Data" permanently clears everything stored on your device. Export a PDF report beforehand if you want a backup copy.

Does the app work for other DOT titles or agencies, not just Highway Repairer?

The app is built primarily around Highway Repairer pay rules, but many core features work for other titles too. If something's title-specific and wrong for your role, let us know — we're actively expanding coverage.

Something else?

If your question isn't covered here, just email timeandahalf@jlunamusic.com and describe what's going on. Screenshots help if it's a display or scanning issue.
